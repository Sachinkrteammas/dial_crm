Privacy Policy

User Data – Definition

“User Data” refers to all confidential information of end-users and all data and information that is:

  1. provided to MAS by or on behalf of users;
  2. obtained, maintained, developed, or generated by MAS in the course of delivering services; or
  3. accessed by MAS while performing such services.

User Data includes, without limitation, information relating to users’ identity, contact information, interactions, transactions, preferences, communications, usage patterns, and any operational, technical, or behavioral data associated with such users.

User Data expressly excludes MAS’s own software, materials, tools, systems, or confidential information.

Role of Data Controller & Processor

The organization that owns or collects the User Data shall remain the sole controller and owner of User Data for the purposes of applicable data privacy and protection laws (“Privacy Laws”).

MAS acknowledges that it acts solely as a data processor of User Data and will comply with all data-processor obligations imposed by applicable Privacy Laws.

MAS will continue to comply with such requirements even if the relevant Privacy Laws are amended or updated.

MAS Security & Safeguards Obligations

MAS will implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect User Data from unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. MAS will maintain appropriate measures to detect, respond to, and report potential data breaches in line with applicable Privacy Laws.

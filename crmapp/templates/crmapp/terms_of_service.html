Terms of Service

A. Nature of MAS’s Business

MAS provides third-party integrated business process outsourcing services, including inbound and outbound customer engagement, blended voice operations, email/chat/IVRS support services, SMS services, knowledge process outsourcing, IT support, and other related telecommunication/IT-enabled activities.

B. MAS Operations

MAS functions as an independent IT-enabled services provider offering call-center operations, database management, CRM/lead management operations, and back-office support services for domestic and international organizations.

C. MAS Capabilities for User Engagement & Acquisition

MAS has the operational expertise, trained manpower, and technology infrastructure required to support organizations in user engagement, lead qualification, business promotion, and acquisition of new users or clientele.

D. MAS Service Delivery Approach

MAS delivers its services through established processes, trained agents, monitoring systems, and technology platforms that facilitate user outreach, user assistance, and data-backed operational support.

E. MAS Delivery Commitments

MAS performs these functions in accordance with the service expectations, operational processes, and compliance requirements communicated by the organization for whom services are executed.
